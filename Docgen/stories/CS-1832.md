Generate configured header/footer, ancillary claims filing instructions and automatic merge values. Visible merge list includes Current Date, Billing Provider Name/Address, Provider City/State/Zip Code, Patient Name, Member ID, Date of Service and Claim Number; the remaining list is clipped.
