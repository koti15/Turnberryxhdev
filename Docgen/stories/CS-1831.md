# CS-1831: Medical Records Reimbursement letter

Updated 2026-10-06. Status: requirement screenshots reviewed; implementation review pending.

## Confirmed requirements

Make the template selectable for an ITS Host provider case under Provider → Other Communication → Print. Generate configured header/footer and merge values: Current Date, Billing Provider Name/Address, Provider City/State/Zip Code, Patient Name, Member ID, Date of Service, Claim Number, Case Number and Patient Account Number. Submit through existing correspondence processing, associate with the case, confirm submission and store correspondence in Alfresco. On failure, do not report a successful print request; allow retry.
